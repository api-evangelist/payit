# PayIt

> PayIt is a customer experience and payments platform for state and local government, founded in 2013 and headquartered in Kansas City, Missouri. Its unified resident profile lets agencies digitize transactions across departments and jurisdictions, serving over 150 million residents across North America. PayIt does not publish a public developer API or documentation surface; it delivers government digital-services and payments capabilities through agency implementations.

## Solutions
- Property Tax, Courts / Tickets & Fines, Utilities, Licensing & Permitting (local government)
- Driver & Vehicle Services, Express Visits, Identity Verification, Insurance Verification (state / DMV)
- Hunting & Fishing Licensing, Volunteer & Event Management (outdoors / conservation)
- Tolling and Motor Vehicle services
- Smart Works, Smart Reconcile, Agent Assistant, Smart Guide (AI-powered platform features)
